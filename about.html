Welcome to Tribal Tourism Portal

The Tribal Tourism Portal helps visitors discover tribal villages, waterfalls, forests, handicrafts, traditional dances, authentic cuisine, and eco-friendly destinations while supporting sustainable tourism and preserving indigenous heritage.

About Tribal Tourism

Tribal Tourism is a form of sustainable tourism that allows visitors to experience the unique traditions, culture, arts, festivals, food, and natural surroundings of indigenous (tribal) communities while respecting their heritage and supporting their economic development. It offers travelers an opportunity to explore tribal villages, interact with local communities, learn about traditional customs, enjoy cultural performances, purchase authentic handicrafts, and experience eco-friendly tourism in forests, hills, and remote landscapes.
