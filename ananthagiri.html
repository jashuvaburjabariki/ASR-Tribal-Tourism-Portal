Welcome to Ananthagiri Hills

The enchanting mountain gateway to the ASR agency tracts. Renowned for rolling coffee plantations, ancient limestone caves, cascading jungle waterfalls, and cool mountain breezes across the Eastern Ghats.

About Ananthagiri Hills

Perched at an elevation of approximately 1,168 meters (3,832 feet) in the Alluri Sitharama Raju District, Ananthagiri Hills is one of the most picturesque hill stations in Andhra Pradesh. Nestled along the scenic winding ghat road between Visakhapatnam and Araku Valley, the region is celebrated as the historic cradle of Andhra's coffee culture, first cultivated here under British supervision in 1898.

Ananthagiri is enveloped by dense Eastern Ghats forests, medicinal plant reserves, and organic coffee plantations sheltered under tall silver oak canopies. It is home to iconic landmarks including the million-year-old Borra Caves, the roaring 100-foot Thatiguda (Tadimada) Waterfalls, the Tyda Jungle Bells Eco-Camp, and sacred temples near mountain springs that give birth to the sacred Gosthani River.

Best Time to Visit

  • October to March: The prime sightseeing season with cool, crisp breezes, pleasant days, misty mornings, and blooming coffee gardens.
  • July to September: Monsoon season brings roaring waterfalls like Thatiguda and Katiki into their most thunderous glory amidst lush green hills.
  • Early Mornings & Evenings: Best for witnessing spectacular fog blankets rolling through the valleys, bird watching, and sunset photography.

Quick Travel Info

  • Location: ASR District, Andhra Pradesh
  • Elevation: 1,168 m (3,832 ft)
  • From Vizag: Approx. 85 km (2 hrs drive)
  • From Araku: Approx. 26 km
  • From Tyda: Approx. 12 km
  • Known For: Coffee Estates, Caves, Waterfalls
  • Weather: Pleasant, breezy & cool year-round

Must Try Tribal Dish

Authentic Tribal Bamboo (Bongu) Chicken

An unforgettable Eastern Ghats culinary specialty: tender chicken infused with indigenous forest spices, stuffed inside raw bamboo stalks, and slow-roasted over wood charcoal embers with zero cooking oil.

Specialty: Bamboo Chicken & Bamboo Biryani Timings: 10:00 AM – 9:30 PM Near Thatiguda Falls & Borra Caves Junction
