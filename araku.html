WELCOME TO ARAKU VALLEY

A peaceful hill station in the Eastern Ghats, famous for coffee plantations, tribal heritage, misty valleys, and scenic landscapes.

About Araku Valley

Araku Valley is one of the most beautiful hill stations in Andhra Pradesh, known for its cool climate, lush greenery, and tribal culture. It is surrounded by dense forests, coffee gardens, waterfalls, and colorful valleys that make it a favorite destination for nature lovers and cultural travelers.

The region is also famous for its indigenous tribal communities, organic coffee, scenic viewpoints, and opportunities for peaceful village experiences. Araku is a perfect place to relax, explore nature, and learn about the traditions of the local tribes.

Best Time to Visit

  • October to February for cool weather and sightseeing
  • June to September for lush green views and monsoon charm
  • Early morning or sunset for beautiful valley views

Quick Info

  • Location: Araku Valley, Andhra Pradesh
  • Distance: Around 115 km from Visakhapatnam
  • Best For: Coffee, valleys, tribal culture
  • Weather: Pleasant and cool all year
