Eastern Ghats Highlands

Welcome to Chinthapalli

An enchanting high-altitude destination nestled in the Eastern Ghats of Alluri Sitarama Raju District. Celebrated for sprawling pine forests, chilly winters, pristine waterfalls, high-altitude agro-tourism, and rich tribal heritage.

About Chinthapalli

Chinthapalli (also known as Chintapalle) is located at an altitude of approximately 839 meters (2,753 feet) above sea level in the Eastern Ghats of ASR District, Andhra Pradesh. Famous for its cool misty mornings, chilly winter breezes, and sprawling pine forests, the town has earned fame as the "Hidden Ooty" of Andhra Pradesh.

The region is deeply tied to the Indian freedom struggle. It was at the historic Chinthapalli police station in August 1922 that the legendary hero Alluri Sitarama Raju launched the historic Rampa Rebellion to defend tribal rights. Today, visitors flock to Chinthapalli to explore organic strawberry orchards, high-altitude agro-tourism research facilities, peaceful coffee plantations, and stunning tiered waterfalls like Kothapalli.

Best Time to Visit

  • October to February: Chilly winter climate (temperatures often drop below 10°C), ideal for mist, fog, and fresh strawberry picking.
  • July to September: Monsoon season transforms the hills into lush green carpets with vibrant, gushing waterfalls.
  • Early Mornings & Evenings: Perfect for experiencing cloud beds, golden sunrises, and tranquil walks in the pine woods.

Quick Travel Info

  • Location: ASR District, Andhra Pradesh
  • Elevation: 839 m (2,753 ft)
  • From Vizag: Approx. 135 km (3.5 hrs drive)
  • From Paderu: Approx. 45 km
  • From Lambasingi: Approx. 30 km
  • Known For: Pine Forest, Strawberry, Waterfalls
  • Weather: Cool & crisp; sharp winter chill

Authentic Tribal Bongu Chicken

Traditional tribal culinary delicacy where farm chicken is marinated in fresh mountain herbs, packed inside green bamboo stalks, and slow-roasted over burning wood embers without a drop of oil.
