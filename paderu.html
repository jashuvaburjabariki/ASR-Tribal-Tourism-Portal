Paderu

A scenic tribal destination in the Eastern Ghats, known for misty hills, cool weather, waterfalls, and rich indigenous culture.

About Paderu

Paderu is one of the most beautiful tribal destinations in Andhra Pradesh, nestled in the Eastern Ghats. Surrounded by dense forests, coffee estates, and rolling hills, it offers a peaceful retreat for travelers seeking nature, heritage, and authentic tribal experiences.

The region is famous for its refreshing climate, local tribal traditions, scenic viewpoints, and waterfalls. Visitors can explore village life, taste local food, enjoy nature walks, and witness colorful ethnic culture.

Best Time to Visit

  • October to February for cool weather and sightseeing
  • June to September for green landscapes and monsoon beauty
  • Early mornings are best for scenic views and photography

Quick Info

  • Location: Paderu, Andhra Pradesh
  • Distance: Around 105 km from Visakhapatnam
  • Best For: Nature, tribal culture, waterfalls
  • Weather: Pleasant and cool throughout the year
